Beengala applies artificial intelligence to real estate. You describe the life you want — how far
from work, how much light, what the street should feel like — and it searches for you, books the
visit and hands the case to an accredited broker, with everything it already knows.

We're building it now and testing it in a closed, invite-only beta, starting in Querétaro, Mexico.

### What we care about

- **One home, one listing.** The same property circulates duplicated and with details that don't
  match. We want each home to exist once, and every claim about it backed by evidence.
- **Brokers' time is real time.** A visit is booked when there is real interest, and the broker
  receives the case already understood.
- **Privacy is not a layer added at the end.** What someone tells us about their life serves their
  own search and nothing else.
